# Authored Icelandic on Orðrétt, for a native reader

Everything below was written for the page with an AI assistant's help, not by a
native speaker. The tables' own words are not here; they are shown exactly as
Samtal published them. Corrections go in `ordrett/page.html` (and `saga.html`),
then `bash ordrett/publish.sh`.

Numbers agree with Icelandic usage in code: 1, 21, 31 … take the singular
(`21 upptaka`, `21 lína`, `21 rödd talar`).
